## Source classification: mixed fleet vs women's championship

Oman Sail publishes a [mixed ILCA 6 fleet table](https://www.omansail.om/events/mussanah-race-week-2026#results) and a separately scored [Arab Women's Championship ILCA 6 table](https://www.omansail.om/events/3rd-arab-women-s-sailing-championship#results). Ines Gmati has five race wins in the 11-entry mixed fleet and seven in the five-entry women's table. The [closing report](https://omansail.om/news/15th-mussanah-race-week-and-3rd-arab-women-s-sailing-championship-conclude-in-spectacular-style-at-barcelo-mussanah-resort) gives seven wins, consistent with the women's results. It is not a race-score conflict once the scoring categories are distinguished. Its use of “overall” for men's standings remains ambiguous; do not treat the men's podium as the mixed-fleet overall podium.
