# Championship award completion — 26 October 2026

Follow-up to the girls' awards audit: 51 additional named award records across seven published scorecards, plus named first-place trophies on the existing Worlds/Asians overall honours. No new sailor identities, birth dates, scores, database writes, or ranking-policy changes.

## Counts and fleet links

- `total` remains the count of all honours. `podiums` is the sum of gold/silver/bronze category placings; a 14th-place award stays visible but does not increase the podium count. Finite positive category rank takes precedence over words such as “Gold Fleet”, “Silver Fleet” or “Vice Champion”. The 14th-place icon is labelled Award, not Medal.
- One sailor may legitimately earn separate open, continental, gender or age-category honours; these remain separate records. A named trophy awarded with the same first-place honour is combined into that honour rather than duplicated.
- ILCA 4 Boys/Girls formerly collapsed to a shared `ilca-4` key. They now have `ilca-4-boys` and `ilca-4-girls` tabs and direct links. Existing Open ILCA 4 sheets retain their class key.
- Exact-edition schedules and division restrictions remain in force. Neither selection trials nor future editions inherit these records. Existing girls' honours are retained.
