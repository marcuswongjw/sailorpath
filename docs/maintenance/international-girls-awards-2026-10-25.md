## Identity and display safeguards

All 21 named girls' award records carry the exact existing profile handle and the event sail number. No names alone or sail numbers alone determine a new identity. European open and closed podiums remain distinct; U18/U16 honours remain distinct. Overall named awards suppress duplicate generic podium honours for that same event/class. Female/age-only awards leave the recorded overall podium available. Separately scored Girls divisions cannot leak to Boys sheets. Each card retains its official source link.
