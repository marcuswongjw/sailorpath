# 2026 Asian Optimist Index: regional qualifier slate

Include **Hong Kong in the programme**, but do not force a Hong Kong event into the first scored index until it has a final, source-backed result and verifiable country data. Build a regional **eligible pool of up to two events per host country**. In the scoring formula, however, only a sailor’s **best result from each host country** may count. This gives sailors more calendar choice without allowing countries with denser regatta calendars to dominate the Asia-wide index.

The 2026 IODA Asian & Oceanian Championship in Colombo remains the required continental anchor. Its official overall final classification contributes 60% of the final index. The qualifier pool provides the remaining 40%. [1]

## Country-cap rule

A country may contribute at most two approved events to the season pool. A sailor may earn activity credit for both events, but their index uses only that sailor’s strongest result from the country. The three qualifying-result slots must come from **three distinct host countries** wherever possible.

For example, if a sailor races both Malaysian events, only their higher Malaysian score may enter the index. The second result remains visible as verified participation and can support the confidence label, but it cannot occupy a second scoring slot. This is the right answer to the “two events per country” question: **yes for coverage; no for double-counting performance.**

Use this first-season formula:

> **2026 Asian Optimist Index = 60% Colombo + 28% best host-country qualifier + 8% second host-country qualifier + 4% third host-country qualifier**

A sailor with verified qualifiers from three countries earns full coverage. A sailor with Colombo plus only one or two countries remains ranked, but receives an Established or Championship-only confidence label. Do not redistribute missing points; that preserves a modest incentive for meaningful regional participation without awarding attendance points.

## What launches now

The practical launch pool is Malaysia, Thailand and Singapore, with **one primary and one alternate per country**. It is enough to start the import and review pipeline, but it should not be described as comprehensive Asian coverage.

Hong Kong should be added now to the regional sailor directory and event watchlist. It joins the first scored season as soon as either Hong Kong Race Week is confirmed final with nationality evidence, or an official individual Hong Kong Optimist championship result is found. That is a better standard than including an incomplete HKG score merely to make the country list look broader.

## Eligibility gates

Every score-bearing event must pass these gates before it becomes active:

1. An organiser, national authority, class association, or official event board publishes a final result.
2. Sailor identity, place, fleet size, races and any discard are visually reconciled from the result source.
3. The index uses one primary classification only: overall final when available, otherwise Gold final. Prize, girls, novice, Silver and Green sheets are profile context, not duplicate inputs.
4. International events need verified representation from at least three nationalities and at least 20 classified sailors to receive a full 0–100 result score.
5. National events may be used where there are at least 20 classified sailors, but their event score is capped at 70. Club/development events remain alternates in the first season.

This model is deliberately expandable. Once HKG has a final qualified event, the index uses the best three distinct-country qualifier results from Malaysia, Thailand, Singapore and Hong Kong. When more countries meet the same source standard, they enter the pool under the same two-event country ceiling.
