The counts are entries, including any entered boat scored DNC; they do not assert that every entrant started a race.

## Scope and limitations

Read-only production queries examined published event/class groups containing multiple sheets and searched result evidence and schedule notes for qualifying/qualification and Q1 labels. Local import reports and manifests were also searched. Separate pre-entered Singapore Gold/Silver fleets, Green/Intermediate fleets, sex divisions and class groups were not combined merely because they share an event. Asian Optimist championships imported as overall sheets already show the full field (2024: 133; 2023: 157). The ILCA 7 Australian sheet already shows its overall field.

This is not a complete source-by-source examination of every historical result: legacy sheets can lack qualifying metadata or source links, and the evidence-text query cannot discover all such cases. The 2026 RSYC Silver knockout has qualification stages within its own 53-boat sheet, not a demonstrated shared Gold/Silver qualifying field. Other undocumented events need official source review before their counts can be combined.
