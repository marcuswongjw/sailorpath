# Malaysian Optimist regattas to scope: 2025–2026

**Start with five archive imports.** They form three recurring Malaysian youth regatta series in Langkawi: the Langkawi International Regatta Perdana (LIRP), the Langkawi International Youth Sailing Championship (LIYSC), and the MSA–NSC–MILO National Open. This is a strong first Malaysia dataset because each event is multi-class but explicitly includes Optimist, runs at a common venue, and attracts Malaysian plus international youth sailors.

## Import now

### 22nd Langkawi International Regatta Perdana 2026

**8–12 February 2026 · National Sailing Centre, Langkawi, Kedah.** The Kedah Sailing Association’s Notice of Race explicitly includes Optimist under-16, Optimist Junior under-12 and under-10, with Optimist overall, divisions A/B/C, girls and novice prizes. It also schedules up to 12 races. [1]

The supplied official [LIRP 2026 Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jmJ5HWFMrOFPxcnMNhN_j-_lyzNJ-0Km) contains `OPTIMIST.pdf`, `OPTIMIST DIV.pdf`, `OPTIMIST GIRLS.pdf`, `OPTIMIST NOVICE.pdf`, and `PRIZES-LIRP2026.pdf`. Import **`OPTIMIST.pdf` as the primary overall class sheet**. Use the category sheets only to visually cross-check scores, ranks and prize slices; do not import them as additional result tables because that would duplicate the same sailors.

### 5th Langkawi International Youth Sailing Championship 2026

**24–28 June 2026 · Langkawi.** The official notice-board record confirms the event and shows Optimist Division A and Division B proceedings, which verifies that Optimist was actively raced. [2]

The supplied official [LIYSC 2026 Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1nRECcJX2V3qEYRvpvXtVI2M8zW3u9z5O) contains an Optimist overall result sheet plus division, girls and novice sheets. Import the overall sheet first, and use the other three as score and category validation. This should be the second live proof point for the new Malaysia workflow.
